# 🚀 LifeOS: The Ultimate Dashboard for Bangladeshi University Students

**LifeOS** is a premium, all-in-one personalized student management web application designed specifically for Bangladeshi varsity students and teenagers. Managing university courses, private tuitions, daily expenses, and leisure tracking can get overwhelming—LifeOS brings everything into a single, ultra-modern dark-themed glassmorphism interface.

🌍 **Live Demo:** https://life-tracker-repository.vercel.app/

---

## ✨ Features Tailored for BD Students

### 🎓 1. Academic Hub (NSU Curriculum Optimized)
* **7-Day Timetable:** Track class schedules seamlessly from Saturday to Friday.
* **Syllabus & Exam Tracker:** Visual progress bars with "Not Started", "In Progress", and "Completed" states for each chapter.
* **Weighted CGPA Calculator:** Specifically built for North South University's grading and credit weight system ($3$ credits vs $1$ credit lab). Includes a **CGPA Goal Simulator**.

### 💸 2. Tuition & Income Manager
* **Multi-Day Scheduler:** Assign multiple days (e.g., Sun, Tue, Thu) to a single tuition student profile.
* **Attendance Counter:** Quick click counters to log classes taken vs remaining.
* **Payment Status Toggle:** Visual glowing indicators for "Paid" or "Pending" monthly salaries.

### 📊 3. Smart Budget & Expense Tracker
* **Daily Ledger:** Log expenses with local context (Food, Transport, Utilities, etc.).
* **Financial Health Alert:** The dashboard applies a pulsing red warning glow across financial elements if spending exceeds 80% of your budget.
* **Net Balance Sheet:** Automatically pipes tuition income to show your real-time cash balance.

### 🎬 4. Entertainment Hub (Movies & Games)
* **Backlog Management:** Keep track of watchlists and active video games.
* **Interactive Completion Archive:** Moving an item to "Completed" opens a custom 5-star rating overlay and journal notes section.

### 🔒 5. Data Privacy & Backup
* Runs completely in your local browser storage (`localStorage`). No online servers or cloud leaks.
* Includes **JSON Data Export & Import** backup buttons to prevent losing data when clearing browser cache.
